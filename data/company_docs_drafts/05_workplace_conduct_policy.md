# NimbusWorks Workplace Conduct Policy
Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Professionalism
### 1.1 General Conduct
All employees must maintain a professional and respectful demeanor in all interactions, both internal and external. Harassment, discrimination, and bullying are strictly prohibited.

### 1.2 Dress Code
NimbusWorks observes a smart-casual dress code in the office. Client-facing roles, such as Sales Executives, should dress in business attire when meeting clients.

## 2. Communication
### 2.1 Internal Communication
Slack is the primary tool for internal communication. Employees should use appropriate channels and observe reasonable response times during core hours.

### 2.2 Social Media
Employees must not disclose confidential company information on social media. Personal opinions expressed online should not be attributed to NimbusWorks.

## 3. Conflict of Interest
### 3.1 Outside Employment
Employees are not permitted to engage in outside employment that conflicts with their duties at NimbusWorks or involves working for a competitor.

### 3.2 Gifts and Entertainment
Employees must not accept gifts exceeding $50 in value from clients, vendors, or partners without prior approval from a Branch/Team Manager.

## 4. Substance Abuse
### 4.1 Alcohol and Drugs
The consumption of alcohol and illegal drugs on company premises is strictly prohibited, except at company-sponsored events where alcohol is authorized.
