# NimbusWorks Sales Standard Operating Procedure
Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Lead Generation
### 1.1 Sourcing Leads
Sales Executives are responsible for sourcing leads via LinkedIn, industry conferences, and inbound marketing funnels managed by Marketing Executives.

### 1.2 Qualification
Leads must be qualified using the BANT (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline) framework before being added to the CRM as an active opportunity.

## 2. The Pitch
### 2.1 Demonstrations
Product demos should be tailored to the prospect's specific pain points in project management or billing. Software Support Engineers may be looped in for technical queries.

### 2.2 Proposal Creation
Proposals must use the official NimbusWorks template and include standard pricing tiers. Custom pricing requires approval from a Branch/Team Manager.

## 3. Closing the Deal
### 3.1 Contract Negotiation
Legal counsel must review any modifications to the standard Master Service Agreement (MSA) requested by the client.

### 3.2 Handoff
Once a contract is signed, the Sales Executive must complete the handoff form in the CRM to transition the account to Customer Support Executives.

## 4. Post-Sale Activity
### 4.1 Follow-up
Sales Executives should check in with new clients 30 days post-launch to ensure satisfaction and identify upsell opportunities.
