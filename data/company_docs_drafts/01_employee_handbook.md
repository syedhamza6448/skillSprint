# NimbusWorks Employee Handbook
Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Welcome to NimbusWorks
### 1.1 Our Mission
NimbusWorks aims to simplify operations for B2B enterprises through cutting-edge project management and billing software. We empower teams to collaborate efficiently and manage finances seamlessly.

### 1.2 Our Values
Our core values are transparency, innovation, accountability, and customer obsession. Every employee, from Sales Executives to DevOps Engineers, is expected to embody these values in their daily operations.

## 2. Employment Basics
### 2.1 Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM in the employee's respective time zone. Core hours where all employees must be available for meetings are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM.

### 2.2 Remote Work
NimbusWorks operates on a hybrid model. Employees are permitted to work remotely up to 3 days a week, subject to approval from their Branch/Team Manager.

## 3. Workplace Environment
### 3.1 Office Safety
All employees must follow safety protocols outlined in our standard operating procedures. Suspicious activities or hazards should be reported immediately.

### 3.2 Equipment Use
Laptops and software licenses provided by NimbusWorks remain company property. They must be used strictly for professional duties.

## 4. Performance and Growth
### 4.1 Performance Reviews
Bi-annual performance reviews take place in June and December. HR Executives oversee the process, while direct managers evaluate performance against set OKRs.

### 4.2 Professional Development
NimbusWorks offers an annual stipend of $1,000 for relevant training, courses, and certifications.
