# NimbusWorks Diversity & Inclusion Policy
Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Commitment to Inclusion
### 1.1 Equal Opportunity Commitment
NimbusWorks fosters a workplace culture built on mutual respect, equal opportunity, and zero tolerance for discrimination based on race, gender, religion, or disability.

### 1.2 Diverse Hiring Practices
HR Executives must ensure job descriptions use inclusive language and candidate shortlists represent diverse applicant pools for all open positions.

## 2. Workplace Accessibility
### 2.1 Accommodations Policy
Employees requiring physical or digital accessibility accommodations should submit a request to the HR Executive team. Accommodations will be fulfilled within 14 days.

### 2.2 Inclusive Facilities
Branch/Team Managers and Operations Coordinators are responsible for ensuring physical office spaces meet standard accessibility guidelines and gender-neutral facility requirements.

## 3. Employee Resource Groups (ERGs)
### 3.1 ERG Charter
NimbusWorks supports Employee Resource Groups (ERGs) to foster community and mentorship. ERGs receive an annual operational budget of $2,000 per group.

### 3.2 Meeting Participation
Employees are permitted up to 2 hours of paid work time per month to participate in approved ERG activities and inclusion workshops.

## 4. Accountability and Reporting
### 4.1 Reporting Discrimination
Incidents of bias, discrimination, or exclusion must be reported immediately to HR Executives or via the anonymous ethics hotline.
