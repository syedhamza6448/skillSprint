# NimbusWorks Finance Standard Operating Procedure
Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Payroll Processing
### 1.1 Timesheets
All hourly employees must submit their timesheets by the 15th of each month. Branch/Team Managers must approve them by the 18th.

### 1.2 Salary Disbursal
Finance Associates process payroll on the 25th to ensure funds reach employees by the 28th. Any delays must be communicated company-wide immediately.

## 2. Expense Reimbursement
### 2.1 Submitting Claims
Employees must submit expense claims via the internal finance portal within 30 days of the transaction, accompanied by valid receipts.

### 2.2 Approval Limits
Branch/Team Managers can approve expenses up to $500. Expenses above this amount require sign-off from the Finance Director.

## 3. Client Invoicing
### 3.1 Invoice Generation
Invoices are generated automatically by the billing system on the 1st of each month. Finance Associates must perform a spot check on 10% of generated invoices.

### 3.2 Outstanding Payments
Clients with invoices overdue by 30 days must receive a reminder. At 60 days overdue, account access will be suspended until payment is received.

## 4. Vendor Payments
### 4.1 Payment Terms
NimbusWorks standard payment terms for vendors are Net 30. Finance Associates must ensure all vendor invoices are logged upon receipt.

### 4.2 Approvals
All vendor payments require a dual-approval process to prevent fraudulent transactions.
