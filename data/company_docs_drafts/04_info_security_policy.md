# NimbusWorks Information Security Policy
Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Access Control
### 1.1 Passwords
Employees must use passwords with a minimum of 14 characters, incorporating numbers and special characters. Passwords must be changed every 90 days.

### 1.2 Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
MFA is mandatory for all NimbusWorks systems, including email, project management software, and internal databases. 

## 2. Data Protection
### 2.1 Encryption
All company devices must have full-disk encryption enabled. Customer billing data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.

### 2.2 Public Wi-Fi
Connecting to public, unsecured Wi-Fi networks is strictly prohibited unless connected through the official NimbusWorks VPN.

## 3. Incident Management
### 3.1 Reporting Security Incidents
Suspected phishing, malware, or lost devices must be reported to the DevOps/IT Engineer team immediately via the designated emergency slack channel.

### 3.2 Response Protocol
The IT team will isolate affected systems within 30 minutes of a reported breach to prevent lateral movement.

## 4. Physical Security
### 4.1 Badges
Office access requires a physical ID badge. Tailgating is prohibited.

### 4.2 Clear Desk Policy
Employees must ensure that no sensitive documents or unlocked devices are left unattended at their workstations.
