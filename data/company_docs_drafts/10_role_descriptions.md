# NimbusWorks Role Descriptions
Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Sales and Marketing
### 1.1 Sales Executive
Responsible for lead generation, conducting software demonstrations, and closing deals for our B2B SaaS platform.

### 1.2 Marketing Executive
Handles inbound marketing funnels, creates content, manages campaigns, and tracks marketing ROI to support the sales pipeline.

## 2. Customer and Software Support
### 2.1 Customer Support Executive
The first point of contact for clients. Resolves general queries, billing issues, and escalates technical problems.

### 2.2 Software Support Engineer
Handles escalated technical issues, diagnoses software bugs, and provides technical workarounds for complex customer problems.

## 3. Operations and HR
### 3.1 HR Executive
Manages recruitment, onboarding, employee relations, performance reviews, and enforces company policies.

### 3.2 Operations Coordinator
Oversees daily administrative tasks, coordinates internal communications, and ensures office facilities operate smoothly.

## 4. Finance and Data
### 4.1 Finance Associate
Processes payroll, handles expense reimbursements, manages client invoicing, and tracks vendor payments.

### 4.2 Data Analyst
Analyzes product usage data, creates business intelligence dashboards, and provides data-driven insights to leadership.

## 5. Engineering and Management
### 5.1 DevOps/IT Engineer
Maintains company infrastructure, enforces information security policies, and provisions IT equipment for employees.

### 5.2 Branch/Team Manager
Leads their respective department, approves leaves and expenses, conducts performance reviews, and sets team objectives.
