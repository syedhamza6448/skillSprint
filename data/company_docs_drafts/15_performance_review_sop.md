# NimbusWorks Performance Review Process Standard Operating Procedure
Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Review Cycle and Timelines
### 1.1 Bi-Annual Review Schedule
Performance reviews take place twice a year: Mid-Year Review in June and End-of-Year Review in December. HR Executives manage the overall evaluation calendar.

### 1.2 Goal Setting (OKRs)
Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) must be established by employees and approved by their Branch/Team Manager during the first two weeks of each half-year cycle.

## 2. Evaluation Components
### 2.1 Self-Assessment
Employees must submit a self-assessment evaluation form in the HR portal at least 7 days before their scheduled review meeting.

### 2.2 Manager Assessment
Branch/Team Managers conduct written performance evaluations assessing core competencies, goal achievement, and adherence to company values.

## 3. Rating Scale and Calibration
### 3.1 Performance Calibration
Departmental performance ratings undergo a calibration session led by HR Executives to ensure consistent and fair scoring across all teams.

### 3.2 Merit and Promotion Alignment
Promotions and merit-based salary adjustments are directly tied to End-of-Year review ratings and require final sign-off from HR Executives and Branch/Team Managers.

## 4. Improvement Plans
### 4.1 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
Employees receiving an unsatisfactory rating are placed on a 60-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) co-developed by the Branch/Team Manager and HR Executive.
