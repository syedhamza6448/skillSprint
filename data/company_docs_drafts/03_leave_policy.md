# NimbusWorks Leave Policy
Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Annual Leave
### 1.1 Entitlement
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. 

### 1.2 Approval Process
Leave requests of 3 days or fewer must be submitted at least one week in advance. Leaves exceeding 3 days require a minimum two-week notice and approval from the Branch/Team Manager.

## 2. Sick Leave
### 2.1 Sick Days
Employees are granted 10 paid sick days annually. Unused sick days do not carry over to the next year.

### 2.2 Medical Certificates
A medical certificate is required if an employee takes 3 or more consecutive sick days.

## 3. Other Types of Leave
### 3.1 Maternity/Paternity Leave
Primary caregivers are eligible for 16 weeks of paid leave. Secondary caregivers are eligible for 4 weeks.

### 3.2 Bereavement Leave
Employees may take up to 5 days of paid leave in the event of the death of an immediate family member.

## 4. Unpaid Leave
### 4.1 Extended Absences
In exceptional circumstances, unpaid leave of up to 3 months may be granted at the discretion of the Branch/Team Manager and HR Executive.
