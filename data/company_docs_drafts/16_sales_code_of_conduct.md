# NimbusWorks Sales Code of Conduct
Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Ethical Sales Practices
### 1.1 Truthful Representation
Sales Executives must accurately represent NimbusWorks SaaS platform features, performance metrics, and pricing. Misrepresenting product capabilities is strictly forbidden.

### 1.2 Anti-Bribery and Corruption
Offering kickbacks, unauthorized discounts, or illegal gifts to prospective clients or procurement officials is strictly prohibited and grounds for immediate termination.

## 2. Prospect Interaction
### 2.1 Respectful Communication
Sales Executives must maintain a professional and non-aggressive approach. Cold call opt-out requests from prospects must be honored within 24 hours.

### 2.2 Competitive Statements
Sales Executives must not make defamatory or unsubstantiated claims regarding competitors' software platforms or business practices.

## 3. Account Allocation and Territory Rules
### 3.1 Lead Ownership Rules
Leads assigned in the CRM belong to the assigned Sales Executive for 30 days. Uncontacted leads after 30 days are automatically reassigned by the Branch/Team Manager.

### 3.2 Territory Boundaries
Sales Executives must respect geographical and enterprise account boundaries. Pitching accounts assigned to another territory requires written manager approval.

## 4. Compliance and Training
### 4.1 Annual Sales Compliance Training
All Sales Executives must complete an annual Sales Compliance and Anti-Corruption training module administered by HR Executives.
