# NimbusWorks Data Privacy Policy
Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Data Collection
### 1.1 Customer Data
NimbusWorks collects customer data solely for providing project management and billing services. Data Analyst and Finance Associate roles must only access data relevant to their tasks.

### 1.2 Employee Data
Employee personal information is collected for payroll, HR, and compliance purposes. It is stored securely and accessible only to HR and Finance teams.

## 2. Data Usage
### 2.1 Permitted Use
Customer data must not be used for marketing without explicit consent. Under no circumstances will NimbusWorks sell customer data to third parties.

### 2.2 Data Anonymization
Data used for product analytics or internal reporting must be anonymized to prevent the identification of individual users.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion
### 3.1 Retention Period
Customer data is retained for the duration of their subscription plus 90 days, after which it is permanently deleted from all active servers.

### 3.2 Deletion Requests
Customers may request immediate data deletion under GDPR/CCPA. The DevOps/IT Engineer team must process these requests within 14 days.

## 4. Breach Notification
### 4.1 Internal Escalation
Any suspected privacy breach must be immediately escalated to the Information Security Officer and the Legal team.

### 4.2 Customer Notification
In the event of a confirmed breach involving sensitive data, affected customers will be notified within 72 hours.
