# NimbusWorks Customer Support Standard Operating Procedure
Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Ticket Management
### 1.1 Triage
Customer Support Executives must monitor the Zendesk queue. Tickets are categorized by severity: Low, Normal, High, and Critical.

### 1.2 Response Times
Critical tickets require an initial response within 30 minutes. All other tickets must be acknowledged within 4 hours during business hours.

## 2. Issue Resolution
### 2.1 First-Contact Resolution
Support agents should strive to resolve issues on the first contact by utilizing the internal knowledge base and standard troubleshooting steps.

### 2.2 Escalation
If a ticket cannot be resolved within 24 hours or involves a software bug, it must be escalated to the Software Support Engineer tier.

## 3. Communication Standards
### 3.1 Tone and Empathy
All customer interactions must be polite, empathetic, and professional. Avoid technical jargon when communicating with non-technical users.

### 3.2 Status Updates
For ongoing issues, customers must receive a status update at least once every 48 hours until the ticket is resolved.

## 4. Quality Assurance
### 4.1 Ticket Audits
Branch/Team Managers will randomly audit 5% of resolved tickets monthly to ensure adherence to quality standards.

### 4.2 Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)
A CSAT survey is automatically sent upon ticket closure. Support Executives are expected to maintain a minimum average score of 4.5/5.0.
