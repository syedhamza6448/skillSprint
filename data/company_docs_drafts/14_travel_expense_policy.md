# NimbusWorks Travel & Expense Policy
Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Business Travel Pre-Approval
### 1.1 Travel Approval
All domestic and international business travel must be pre-approved in writing by the employee's Branch/Team Manager at least 14 days prior to departure.

### 1.2 Booking Channels
Flights and accommodation must be booked through the official company travel portal managed by the Operations Coordinator.

## 2. Daily Allowances and Meals
### 2.1 Meal Per Diem
Employees on business travel are eligible for a maximum meal per diem of $75 per day. Itemized receipts are required for all meal expense claims.

### 2.2 Lodging Limits
Standard hotel accommodation cap is $200 per night for domestic travel and $300 per night for international travel. Higher rates require Finance Associate pre-approval.

## 3. Transportation Expenses
### 3.1 Car Rental and Rideshare
Rideshare services (Uber/Lyft) and standard car rentals are reimbursable for official travel. Rental cars must include collision damage waiver insurance.

### 3.2 Mileage Reimbursement
Personal vehicle usage for business travel is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile. Commuting from home to the primary office is not reimbursable.

## 4. Expense Submission
### 4.1 Submission Timeline
Travel expense reports must be submitted to the Finance Associate team within 15 days of returning from travel. Claims submitted after 30 days will be rejected.
