# NimbusWorks Human Resources Policy
Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Hiring and Onboarding
### 1.1 Recruitment
NimbusWorks is an equal opportunity employer. All recruitment is handled by the HR Executive team using standardized assessment rubrics.

### 1.2 Onboarding Process
New hires must complete a 2-week onboarding program covering company culture, security practices, and role-specific training.

## 2. Compensation and Benefits
### 2.1 Salary Dispersal
Salaries are paid on the 28th of every month. The Finance Associate team processes payroll; any discrepancies should be directed to them by the 20th.

### 2.2 Health Benefits
All full-time employees are enrolled in the company health insurance plan upon completion of their 90-day probationary period.

## 3. Disciplinary Actions
### 3.1 Progressive Discipline
NimbusWorks follows a progressive discipline approach: verbal warning, written warning, final warning, and termination.

### 3.2 Immediate Dismissal
Gross misconduct, including theft, fraud, violence, or severe breach of the Data Privacy Policy, will result in immediate termination without progressive steps.

## 4. Employee Exit
### 4.1 Resignation
Employees must provide a minimum 2-week notice period when resigning. Branch/Team Managers must immediately notify HR.

### 4.2 Offboarding
Departing employees must return all company equipment and participate in an exit interview with an HR Executive.
