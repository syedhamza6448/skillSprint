# NimbusWorks Data Retention Policy
Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Scope and Principles
### 1.1 Policy Objective
This policy defines the retention schedules and mandatory disposal procedures for all physical and digital records generated or held by NimbusWorks.

### 1.2 Compliance Standards
Data retention practices must comply with applicable data protection laws, including GDPR, CCPA, and SOC 2 requirements.

## 2. Data Categories and Retention Schedules
### 2.1 Financial and Tax Records
Finance Associates must retain client invoices, tax filings, payroll records, and financial statements for a minimum of 7 years before archival or destruction.

### 2.2 System and Audit Logs
DevOps/IT Engineers must maintain network security logs, access logs, and application audit trails for at least 365 days.

## 3. Backup and Archival
### 3.1 Backup Retention
System backup snapshots created by DevOps/IT Engineers are retained for 30 days on encrypted cloud storage before automated overwriting.

### 3.2 Legacy Customer Archives
Archived data for inactive client accounts must be isolated in cold storage and permanently deleted after 180 days.

## 4. Secure Destruction
### 4.1 Digital Data Wiping
Digital media slated for decommissioning must undergo cryptographic erasure or physical destruction verified by a DevOps/IT Engineer.
