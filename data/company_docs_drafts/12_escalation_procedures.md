# NimbusWorks Escalation Procedures
Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Customer Support Escalations
### 1.1 Technical Issues
If a Customer Support Executive cannot resolve a ticket within 24 hours, or if it involves a bug, it must be escalated to a Software Support Engineer.

### 1.2 High-Priority Clients
Issues affecting Enterprise-tier clients must be flagged immediately to a Branch/Team Manager, bypassing standard wait times.

## 2. Security and IT Escalations
### 2.1 Data Breaches
Any suspected or confirmed data breach must be reported immediately to the DevOps/IT Engineer team and the Legal department. Do not delay reporting.

### 2.2 System Outages
In the event of a platform outage, the DevOps/IT Engineer team will initiate the Critical Incident Response protocol and notify all Branch/Team Managers within 15 minutes.

## 3. Human Resources Escalations
### 3.1 Workplace Conflict
Interpersonal conflicts that cannot be resolved directly should be escalated to the HR Executive team for mediation.

### 3.2 Policy Violations
Severe violations of the Workplace Conduct Policy or Information Security Policy must be reported to HR and the respective Branch/Team Manager immediately.

## 4. Financial Escalations
### 4.1 Payment Disputes
Client payment disputes exceeding $5,000 must be escalated by the Finance Associate to the Finance Director for resolution.

### 4.2 Fraud Suspicions
Any suspected fraudulent vendor invoices or expense claims must be frozen and escalated to the Finance Director and Legal team.
