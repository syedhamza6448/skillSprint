# NimbusWorks Employee FAQs
Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. General Questions
### 1.1 What are our core working hours?
Our core hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in your local time zone. During this time, all employees should be available for meetings and collaboration.

### 1.2 How many days can I work remotely?
NimbusWorks offers a hybrid model allowing up to 3 days of remote work per week, subject to approval from your Branch/Team Manager.

## 2. Benefits and Leave
### 2.1 When do my health benefits start?
You are eligible to enroll in the company health insurance plan after successfully completing your 90-day probationary period.

### 2.2 Do unused sick days roll over?
No, the 10 paid sick days provided annually do not carry over to the next calendar year.

## 3. Equipment and Security
### 3.1 Can I use my company laptop for personal tasks?
No, company-provided laptops and software licenses must be used strictly for professional duties as per the Information Security Policy.

### 3.2 Who do I contact for IT support?
Please reach out to the DevOps/IT Engineer team via the designated IT helpdesk Slack channel for any hardware or software issues.

## 4. Performance and Expenses
### 4.1 When are performance reviews held?
Bi-annual performance reviews are conducted every June and December.

### 4.2 How do I get reimbursed for a business expense?
Submit your claim with receipts via the internal finance portal within 30 days of the transaction.
