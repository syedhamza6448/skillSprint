# NimbusWorks IT Equipment Policy
Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

## 1. Hardware Allocation
### 1.1 Standard Issue
All full-time employees are provided with a company laptop, monitor, and standard peripherals upon joining. DevOps/IT Engineers oversee the allocation and provisioning.

### 1.2 Asset Tagging
All hardware must be assigned a unique asset tag prior to deployment. Employees must not remove or tamper with physical asset tags.

## 2. Maintenance and Support
### 2.1 Hardware Maintenance
Employees are required to bring company devices to the IT department annually for hardware diagnostics and preventative maintenance.

### 2.2 Repairs and Replacements
Damaged or malfunctioning hardware must be reported to a DevOps/IT Engineer within 24 hours. Unauthorized third-party repairs are strictly prohibited.

## 3. Acceptable Use
### 3.1 Software Installation
Employees may only install software from the pre-approved company software repository. Installing unauthorized third-party software is prohibited.

### 3.2 Personal Use
Company hardware is intended for business purposes. Minimal personal use is permitted during non-working hours, provided it does not violate security policies.

## 4. Hardware Return
### 4.1 Asset Return on Offboarding
Upon termination or resignation, all company-owned IT assets must be returned to the IT department within 3 business days of the last working date.
