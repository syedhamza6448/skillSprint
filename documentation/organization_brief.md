# NexaCore Solutions — Organization Brief

## Company Overview

**Company Name:** NexaCore Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
**Industry:** Mid-size B2B SaaS (Customer Experience & CRM Software)
**Founded:** 2015
**Headquarters:** Karachi, Pakistan (with offices in Lahore and Islamabad)
**Employees:** ~350
**Revenue:** PKR 1.2 billion annually
**Clients:** 200+ mid-to-enterprise-size companies across retail, banking, logistics, and telecom sectors

NexaCore Solutions develops cloud-based CRM and customer experience software. Its flagship product, **NexaCRM Pro**, is used by companies to manage customer data, support tickets, sales pipelines, and analytics. The company operates on a SaaS subscription model with tiered pricing.

## Company Culture & Values

- **Customer First:** Every decision is measured by customer impact.
- **Integrity:** Transparent dealings with clients, employees, and partners.
- **Continuous Learning:** Employees are encouraged to upskill through the internal LMS.
- **Collaboration:** Cross-functional teams work together on product and client success.
- **Data-Driven:** Decisions backed by metrics, not assumptions.

## Key Policies & Compliance Notes

- NexaCore follows SECP (Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan) regulations.
- All employees must adhere to the PECO Data Privacy Act compliance framework.
- The company enforces a strict Information Security Policy aligned with ISO 27001 principles.
- Leave entitlements are governed by the NexaCore HR Policy v2 (superseding v1, effective 2024-01-01).
- Code of conduct is governed by the NexaCore Workplace Conduct Policy.
