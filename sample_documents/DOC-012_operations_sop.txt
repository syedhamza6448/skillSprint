NEXACORE SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
OPERATIONS DEPARTMENT STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)
Document ID: DOC-012
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2023-01-01

=============================================================
SECTION 1: PURPOSE
=============================================================

1.1 This SOP defines the standard processes for the Operations department.

=============================================================
SECTION 2: WORKING HOURS (OPERATIONS-SPECIFIC)
=============================================================

2.1 Operations Coordinators are required to be available from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM on weekdays.

NOTE [CONFLICT-001-REFERENCE]: The Company Handbook (DOC-001) states standard hours end at 6:00 PM. For Operations Coordinators, this departmental SOP takes precedence and extends availability to 7:00 PM.

2.2 Operations staff participate in an on-call rotation for after-hours emergencies.
2.3 Overtime for hours worked beyond 8 hours per day is compensated per the HR Policy (DOC-003).

=============================================================
SECTION 3: VENDOR & SUPPLIER MANAGEMENT
=============================================================

3.1 All vendor contracts must be reviewed and renewed by the Operations team annually.
3.2 New vendors must be evaluated against the NexaCore Vendor Assessment Checklist before engagement.
3.3 Vendor performance reviews are conducted quarterly.

=============================================================
SECTION 4: FACILITY MANAGEMENT
=============================================================

4.1 Operations Coordinators are responsible for maintaining office supplies at adequate levels.
4.2 Maintenance requests must be logged in the Facilities Management System within 24 hours of identifying an issue.
4.3 Emergency maintenance (affecting operations or safety) must be reported to the Operations Manager immediately.

=============================================================
SECTION 5: LOGISTICS & PROCUREMENT
=============================================================

5.1 All procurement requests must be submitted via the purchase request system.
5.2 Procurement requests above PKR 50,000 require Operations Manager approval.
5.3 Requests above PKR 200,000 require CFO approval.
5.4 Preferred vendor lists are maintained by the Operations team and must be consulted before approaching new vendors.

=============================================================
SECTION 6: PERFORMANCE METRICS
=============================================================

6.1 Operations Coordinators are evaluated on:
    - Procurement cycle time (target: under 5 business days for standard requests)
    - Vendor SLA compliance rate (target: 90%)
    - Facilities maintenance response time (target: 98% of requests resolved within SLA)
