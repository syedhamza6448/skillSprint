=============================================================
SECTION 1: ANNUAL LEAVE [CURRENT]
=============================================================

1.1 All employees are entitled to 14 days of annual leave per calendar year after completing 6 months of continuous service.
1.2 During probation (first 3 months), no annual leave is accrued.
1.3 From Month 4 to Month 6, employees accrue leave at 1 day per month.
1.4 Annual leave must be approved by the line manager at least 5 business days in advance.
1.5 A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried forward to Q1 of the next year.
1.6 Carried-forward leave not utilized by March 31 of the following year is forfeited.

=============================================================
SECTION 2: SICK LEAVE [CURRENT]
=============================================================

2.1 Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
2.2 Sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered physician.
2.3 Sick leave may not be converted to cash or carried forward.
2.4 Abuse of sick leave (e.g., repeated single-day absences without medical evidence) is subject to disciplinary review.

=============================================================
SECTION 3: MATERNITY / PATERNITY LEAVE [CURRENT]
=============================================================

3.1 Female employees are entitled to 16 weeks (112 days) of paid maternity leave.
3.2 Paternity leave: 7 calendar days of paid leave for male employees, to be taken within 4 weeks of the birth.
3.3 Adoption leave: 8 weeks paid leave for primary caregivers adopting a child under age 5.

=============================================================
SECTION 4: PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
=============================================================

4.1 NexaCore observes all public holidays declared by the Government of Pakistan.
4.2 If a public holiday falls on a weekend, a compensatory day off is granted on the next working day.
4.3 Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off within 30 days.

=============================================================
SECTION 5: LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)
=============================================================

5.1 Employees who exhaust their leave balance may apply for leave without pay (LWP).
5.2 LWP must be approved by the Department Head and HR Director.
5.3 LWP exceeding 30 consecutive days may affect benefits entitlement.
