NEXACORE SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
CUSTOMER SUPPORT STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)
Document ID: DOC-010
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2023-01-01

=============================================================
SECTION 1: PURPOSE
=============================================================

1.1 This SOP defines the standard processes for the Customer Support & Success department at NexaCore Solutions.

=============================================================
SECTION 2: TICKET HANDLING PROCEDURE
=============================================================

2.1 All customer support tickets are received via email, phone, or the NexaCRM Pro client portal.
2.2 Tickets are categorized by severity:
    - P1 (Critical): System down, data loss risk — response within 1 hour, resolution target 4 hours
    - P2 (High): Major feature broken — response within 4 hours, resolution target 24 hours
    - P3 (Medium): Minor issue, workaround available — response within 24 hours, resolution target 72 hours
    - P4 (Low): General inquiry, cosmetic issue — response within 48 hours

2.3 All tickets must be logged in NexaCRM Pro within 30 minutes of receipt.

NOTE [CONFLICT-009]: The Company Handbook (DOC-001) states customer emails must be responded to within 24 hours as a general standard. The Customer Support SOP (DOC-010) defines more granular SLA-based response times by severity. DOC-010 takes precedence for Support staff.

=============================================================
SECTION 3: ESCALATION PATH
=============================================================

3.1 P1 tickets must be escalated to the Senior Support Engineer and Team Leader immediately upon receipt.
3.2 P2 tickets unresolved after 12 hours must be escalated to Team Leader.
3.3 Any client threatening legal action must be escalated to the Support Manager and Legal team immediately.
3.4 Recurring issues (same client, 3+ P2/P3 tickets in 30 days) must be flagged for a Customer Success Review.

=============================================================
SECTION 4: COMMUNICATION STANDARDS
=============================================================

4.1 All client communications must be professional, empathetic, and solution-oriented.
4.2 Use of slang, casual language, or emoji in client communications is not permitted.
4.3 Call recordings may be reviewed for quality assurance. Employees consent to this monitoring upon employment.
4.4 Hold time on phone calls must not exceed 3 minutes without providing an update to the client.

=============================================================
SECTION 5: KNOWLEDGE BASE MAINTENANCE
=============================================================

5.1 Support Executives must document solutions for novel issues in the NexaCore internal knowledge base within 24 hours of resolution.
5.2 Knowledge base articles must be reviewed and updated quarterly.

=============================================================
SECTION 6: PERFORMANCE METRICS
=============================================================

6.1 Customer Support Executives are evaluated on:
    - First Contact Resolution Rate (target: 75%)
    - Customer Satisfaction Score / CSAT (target: 4.2/5.0)
    - Average Handle Time
    - SLA Compliance Rate (target: 95%)
