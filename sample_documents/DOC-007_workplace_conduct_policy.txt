NEXACORE SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
WORKPLACE CONDUCT POLICY
Document ID: DOC-007
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2023-01-15

=============================================================
SECTION 1: PURPOSE
=============================================================

1.1 NexaCore is committed to maintaining a respectful, inclusive, and professional workplace for all employees.
1.2 This policy applies to all employees, contractors, interns, and visitors on company premises or participating in company activities.

=============================================================
SECTION 2: PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT
=============================================================

2.1 All employees must treat colleagues, clients, and visitors with dignity and respect.
2.2 Discriminatory behavior, harassment, bullying, or intimidation on any basis (gender, religion, ethnicity, age, disability, etc.) is strictly prohibited and is grounds for immediate termination.
2.3 Employees must maintain professional communication standards in all company channels (email, Slack, meetings, client calls).
2.4 Punctuality is expected. Habitual tardiness (3+ occurrences per month) will trigger a formal HR review.

=============================================================
SECTION 3: CONFLICT OF INTEREST
=============================================================

3.1 Employees must disclose any personal, financial, or business interests that conflict or could appear to conflict with their duties at NexaCore.
3.2 Employees may not work for or invest in a direct competitor of NexaCore without prior written approval from the HR Director.
3.3 Accepting gifts from clients or vendors with a value exceeding PKR 2,000 must be disclosed to HR.

=============================================================
SECTION 4: ANTI-BRIBERY & CORRUPTION
=============================================================

4.1 NexaCore has a zero-tolerance policy toward bribery and corruption.
4.2 Employees must never offer or accept bribes, kickbacks, or improper payments of any kind.
4.3 Any requests for bribes from clients or officials must be reported to the Legal & Compliance team immediately.

=============================================================
SECTION 5: SOCIAL MEDIA & EXTERNAL COMMUNICATIONS
=============================================================

5.1 Employees must not post confidential company information, client data, or internal communications on personal social media.
5.2 Employees speaking publicly about NexaCore (e.g., interviews, blog posts) must obtain prior approval from the Marketing Department.
5.3 Negative posts about clients, colleagues, or management on social media are subject to disciplinary action.

=============================================================
SECTION 6: DRESS CODE
=============================================================

6.1 Business casual attire is expected Monday through Thursday.
6.2 Casual Friday is permitted; clothing must still be professional and non-offensive.
6.3 Client-facing meetings require formal business attire unless otherwise specified.

=============================================================
SECTION 7: REPORTING VIOLATIONS
=============================================================

7.1 Employees are encouraged to report policy violations without fear of retaliation.
7.2 Reports can be made to the line manager, HR, or via the anonymous ethics hotline.
7.3 Retaliation against an employee for good-faith reporting is itself a disciplinary offense.
