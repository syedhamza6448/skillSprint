NEXACORE SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
ESCALATION PROCEDURE MANUAL
Document ID: DOC-022
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2023-06-01

=============================================================
SECTION 1: PURPOSE
=============================================================

1.1 This manual defines escalation paths and procedures across all departments to ensure critical issues are addressed promptly and by the appropriate authority.

=============================================================
SECTION 2: CUSTOMER SUPPORT ESCALATION
=============================================================

2.1 P1 (Critical) client issues:
    Step 1: Customer Support Executive logs ticket and immediately alerts Senior Support Engineer
    Step 2: Senior Support Engineer takes ownership and notifies Team Leader within 30 minutes
    Step 3: If unresolved in 2 hours, escalate to Software Support Engineer team
    Step 4: If unresolved in 4 hours, escalate to Customer Support Manager and notify client Account Manager
    Step 5: If unresolved in 8 hours, escalate to CTO and Regional Director

2.2 Clients threatening legal action: Immediate escalation to Support Manager + Legal team, regardless of ticket severity.

NOTE [CONFLICT-012]: The Customer Support SOP (DOC-010) states P1 response is required within 1 hour. The Escalation Procedure Manual (DOC-022) describes a 2-hour Software Support escalation trigger. Both requirements apply independently: the 1-hour response SLA from DOC-010 and the 2-hour escalation trigger from DOC-022 are not in conflict — the 1-hour response is a client communication requirement, while the 2-hour trigger is the internal escalation threshold.

=============================================================
SECTION 3: FINANCIAL ESCALATION
=============================================================

3.1 Invoice disputes above PKR 500,000: Finance Manager + CFO must be notified within 1 business day.
3.2 Suspected financial fraud: Finance Manager + CEO must be notified immediately; do not attempt to resolve internally.
3.3 Tax compliance issues: Notify Finance Manager and Legal team immediately.

=============================================================
SECTION 4: HR ESCALATION
=============================================================

4.1 Harassment complaints: HR Executive escalates to HR Director within 2 hours of receiving the complaint.
4.2 Termination decisions: Must be approved by HR Director and relevant Department Head before communication to employee.
4.3 Disputes involving a Department Head: Escalate to HR Director + CEO.

=============================================================
SECTION 5: SECURITY INCIDENT ESCALATION
=============================================================

5.1 Suspected data breach:
    Step 1: Employee reports to IT Security within 1 hour (per DOC-006)
    Step 2: IT Security notifies DPO and IT Manager immediately
    Step 3: DPO assesses breach severity and notifies CEO and Legal team within 2 hours
    Step 4: If client data is affected, notify affected clients within 24 hours
    Step 5: Regulatory notification as required by law (within 72 hours per DOC-008)

=============================================================
SECTION 6: OPERATIONS ESCALATION
=============================================================

6.1 Vendor non-performance affecting business continuity: Escalate to Operations Manager + CFO within 4 business hours.
6.2 Facility emergency (safety risk): Immediately notify Operations Manager and designated Safety Officer; call emergency services if required.
