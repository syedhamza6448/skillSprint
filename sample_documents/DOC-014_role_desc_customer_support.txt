NEXACORE SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
ROLE DESCRIPTION — CUSTOMER SUPPORT EXECUTIVE
Document ID: DOC-014
Version: 1.0
Role ID: R02
Department: Customer Support & Success

=============================================================
SECTION 1: ROLE SUMMARY
=============================================================

The Customer Support Executive is the primary point of contact for NexaCore's clients, handling technical inquiries, product support, and issue resolution via phone, email, and the NexaCRM Pro client portal.

=============================================================
SECTION 2: KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
=============================================================

- Handle inbound client support tickets across all severity levels (P1-P4)
- Log and categorize all tickets in NexaCRM Pro within 30 minutes of receipt
- Resolve technical issues within defined SLA timeframes
- Escalate unresolved P1/P2 tickets to Senior Support Engineer or Team Leader
- Document solutions for novel issues in the internal knowledge base
- Participate in quarterly knowledge base reviews
- Conduct client satisfaction follow-ups post-resolution
- Maintain CSAT score above 4.2/5.0

=============================================================
SECTION 3: MANDATORY COMPETENCIES
=============================================================

- Strong technical aptitude (familiarity with SaaS products)
- NexaCRM Pro product knowledge (training required within 30 days)
- Professional written and verbal communication skills
- Understanding of ITIL or equivalent service management framework (training within 60 days)
- Empathy and problem-solving orientation

=============================================================
SECTION 4: MANDATORY ONBOARDING REQUIREMENTS
=============================================================

- Company orientation (Day 1)
- NexaCRM Pro product and portal training (Week 1-3)
- Customer Support SOP and SLA training (Week 1)
- Ticket categorization and escalation path training (Week 1)
- Communication standards training (Week 2)
- Information security and client data handling training (Week 1)
- Code of conduct acknowledgment (Day 1)
- Knowledge base usage and contribution training (Week 2)
- Supervised live ticket handling (Weeks 3-4, minimum 20 tickets)
- Support KPI assessment (Week 4)

=============================================================
SECTION 5: KPIs
=============================================================

- First Contact Resolution Rate (target: 75%)
- CSAT Score (target: 4.2/5.0)
- SLA Compliance Rate (target: 95%)
- Ticket logging timeliness (target: 100% within 30 minutes)
