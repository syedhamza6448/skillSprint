SECTION 1: WELCOME TO NEXACORE SOLUTIONS
=============================================================

Welcome to NexaCore Solutions Pvt. Ltd. This handbook outlines our company policies, values, and expectations for all employees. Please read it carefully and retain it for reference.

NexaCore Solutions was founded in 2015 with the mission to help businesses deliver exceptional customer experiences through intelligent CRM software. Our flagship product, NexaCRM Pro, serves over 200 enterprise clients.

=============================================================
SECTION 2: COMPANY VALUES
=============================================================

2.1 Customer First
Every action we take is measured by the impact it has on our clients and their customers.

2.2 Integrity
We operate with full transparency. Employees must not misrepresent information to clients, colleagues, or management.

2.3 Continuous Learning
All employees are encouraged to participate in the NexaCore Learning Portal and pursue relevant certifications.

2.4 Collaboration
We believe the best solutions arise when teams work together across departments.

2.5 Data-Driven Decision Making
Metrics and data are the foundation of every major decision at NexaCore.

=============================================================
SECTION 3: EMPLOYMENT POLICIES
=============================================================

3.1 Working Hours
Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. A one-hour lunch break is provided from 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM.

NOTE [CONFLICT-001]: Section 3.1 states working hours end at 6:00 PM. However, the Operations Department SOP (DOC-012) states that Operations Coordinators must be available until 7:00 PM on weekdays. The more specific departmental SOP takes precedence for Operations staff.

3.2 Probation Period
All new employees undergo a 3-month probationary period. During this period, either party may terminate the employment agreement with 7 days written notice.

NOTE [CONFLICT-002]: The HR Policy Manual v1 (DOC-002) states the probation period is 6 months. This has been revised to 3 months in v2 (DOC-003). The current policy (DOC-003) supersedes DOC-002.

3.3 Annual Leave
Employees are entitled to 14 days of annual leave per year after completing 6 months of service.

NOTE [CONFLICT-003]: The Leave Policy v1 (DOC-004) states employees receive 10 annual leave days. The Leave Policy v2 (DOC-005) states 14 days. DOC-005 supersedes DOC-004 effective 2024-01-01.

3.4 Code of Conduct
All employees must adhere to the Workplace Conduct Policy (DOC-007). Any breach of conduct will be subject to disciplinary action as outlined in Section 7.

=============================================================
SECTION 4: ONBOARDING REQUIREMENTS
=============================================================

4.1 Mandatory Training (All Roles)
All new employees, regardless of role or department, must complete the following within their first 30 days:
- Company orientation session (Day 1)
- Information security awareness training (Week 1)
- Data privacy and compliance briefing (Week 1)
- Code of conduct acknowledgment (Day 1)
- NexaCRM Pro product overview (Week 2)
- Communication tools training (Week 1)
- HR systems and payroll orientation (Week 2)

4.2 Role-Specific Training
Role-specific training requirements are defined in the Role Requirement Matrix and the relevant role description documents.

4.3 Assessment Requirements
All mandatory training modules must be completed with a minimum passing score of 70% on the associated assessments.

=============================================================
SECTION 5: INFORMATION SECURITY
=============================================================

5.1 Device Policy
All company-issued devices must be password-protected. Employees must not install unauthorized software on company devices.

5.2 Data Access
Access to client data is granted on a need-to-know basis. Sharing client data with unauthorized personnel is a termination-level offense.

5.3 Password Policy
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long, include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters. Passwords must be changed every 90 days.

=============================================================
SECTION 6: PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
=============================================================

6.1 Performance Reviews
All employees receive a formal performance review every 6 months (at end of probation and annually thereafter).

6.2 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Each role has defined KPIs. Employees must familiarize themselves with their role-specific KPIs during onboarding.

=============================================================
SECTION 7: DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURE
=============================================================

Disciplinary actions range from verbal warnings to termination, depending on the severity of the breach. All disciplinary actions are documented and stored in the employee's HR file.

=============================================================
SECTION 8: GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE
=============================================================

Employees may raise grievances with their direct manager or with the HR department. Anonymous reports can also be submitted via the company's ethics hotline.
