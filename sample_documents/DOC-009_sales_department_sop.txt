NEXACORE SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
SALES DEPARTMENT STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)
Document ID: DOC-009
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2023-01-01

=============================================================
SECTION 1: PURPOSE
=============================================================

1.1 This SOP defines the standard processes for the Sales & Business Development department at NexaCore Solutions.
1.2 All Sales Executives and Business Development staff must follow this SOP.

=============================================================
SECTION 2: LEAD MANAGEMENT PROCESS
=============================================================

2.1 All leads must be entered into NexaCRM Pro within 24 hours of first contact.
2.2 Leads are categorized as: Cold, Warm, Hot.
2.3 Hot leads must be followed up within 2 business hours.
2.4 Warm leads must be followed up within 1 business day.
2.5 Cold leads enter the automated email nurture sequence and are reviewed monthly.

=============================================================
SECTION 3: SALES PIPELINE STAGES
=============================================================

3.1 The NexaCore sales pipeline consists of 6 stages:
    Stage 1: Prospecting
    Stage 2: Qualification
    Stage 3: Discovery & Demo
    Stage 4: Proposal Sent
    Stage 5: Negotiation
    Stage 6: Closed Won / Closed Lost

3.2 Sales Executives must update the pipeline stage in NexaCRM Pro within 24 hours of any stage change.
3.3 Any deal stagnating in Stage 3 or beyond for more than 14 days must be escalated to the Sales Team Leader.

NOTE [CONFLICT-008]: The Sales Team Leader SOP states that escalation is required after 10 days in Stage 3. The Sales Department SOP (DOC-009) states 14 days. For Sales Executives, the Team Leader SOP takes precedence for their department.

=============================================================
SECTION 4: PROPOSAL & QUOTATION PROCEDURE
=============================================================

4.1 All proposals must use the approved NexaCore proposal template (available on the company intranet).
4.2 Pricing discounts exceeding 15% require approval from the Sales Manager.
4.3 Discounts exceeding 25% require approval from the Sales Director.
4.4 No verbal pricing commitments may be made before the proposal is formally approved.
4.5 Proposals must be sent via the official company email account and through NexaCRM Pro.

=============================================================
SECTION 5: CLIENT ONBOARDING HANDOFF
=============================================================

5.1 Upon closing a deal, the Sales Executive must complete the 'Client Onboarding Handoff' form in NexaCRM Pro.
5.2 The handoff must include: client contact details, agreed service scope, contract start date, SLA tier, and technical requirements.
5.3 The handoff must occur within 2 business days of the contract being signed.
5.4 The Account Manager / Customer Success team must be introduced to the client via email within the same 2-day window.

=============================================================
SECTION 6: REPORTING REQUIREMENTS
=============================================================

6.1 Sales Executives must submit a weekly activity report every Friday by 4:00 PM.
6.2 Monthly pipeline reviews are held on the first Monday of each month.
6.3 Quarterly business reviews (QBRs) are mandatory for all Sales staff.
