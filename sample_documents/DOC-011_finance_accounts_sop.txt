NEXACORE SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
FINANCE & ACCOUNTS STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)
Document ID: DOC-011
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2023-01-01

=============================================================
SECTION 1: PURPOSE
=============================================================

1.1 This SOP defines standard processes for the Finance & Accounts department.
1.2 All Finance Associates and Accounts staff must adhere to this SOP.

=============================================================
SECTION 2: ACCOUNTS PAYABLE
=============================================================

2.1 All vendor invoices must be received and logged within 24 hours of arrival.
2.2 Payment terms are Net 30 unless otherwise specified in the vendor contract.
2.3 Invoices above PKR 100,000 require dual authorization (Finance Associate + Finance Manager).
2.4 Invoices above PKR 500,000 require triple authorization (Finance Associate + Finance Manager + CFO).
2.5 All payments must be processed through the approved company banking portal. No cash payments for amounts exceeding PKR 5,000.

NOTE [CONFLICT-010]: The Company Handbook (DOC-001) states payments over PKR 50,000 require manager approval. The Finance SOP (DOC-011) sets the threshold at PKR 100,000 with dual authorization. The Finance SOP, as a department-specific document, takes precedence for Finance staff.

=============================================================
SECTION 3: ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE
=============================================================

3.1 Invoices to clients must be issued within 3 business days of service delivery or milestone completion.
3.2 Overdue invoices (30+ days) must be escalated to the Finance Manager for follow-up.
3.3 Clients with invoices 60+ days overdue are placed on a credit hold and may not receive new services until payment is received.

=============================================================
SECTION 4: EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENTS
=============================================================

4.1 Employee expense claims must be submitted within 10 business days of incurring the expense.
4.2 Receipts are required for all expenses above PKR 500.
4.3 Travel expenses require prior approval from the line manager before travel.
4.4 Reimbursements are processed in the monthly payroll cycle.

=============================================================
SECTION 5: FINANCIAL REPORTING
=============================================================

5.1 Monthly financial reports must be submitted to the CFO by the 5th of the following month.
5.2 Quarterly reports are submitted to the Board of Directors.
5.3 Annual audited financial statements must be prepared by an external auditor.

=============================================================
SECTION 6: TAX COMPLIANCE
=============================================================

6.1 All tax filings must be submitted on time to the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR).
6.2 Finance Associates must stay current on withholding tax rates and GST regulations.
6.3 Tax training is mandatory for all Finance staff and must be completed within the first 30 days.
