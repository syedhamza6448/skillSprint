NEXACORE SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Document ID: DOC-006
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2023-06-01

=============================================================
SECTION 1: PURPOSE & SCOPE
=============================================================

1.1 This policy defines NexaCore's requirements for protecting information assets, client data, and internal systems.
1.2 This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors with access to NexaCore systems.

=============================================================
SECTION 2: DEVICE & ACCESS SECURITY
=============================================================

2.1 All company-issued laptops and mobile devices must be encrypted using BitLocker or equivalent.
2.2 Employees must lock their workstations when leaving their desk (Win+L shortcut or equivalent).
2.3 Personal devices may only access company email via the approved MDM (Mobile Device Management) application.
2.4 USB drives and external storage are prohibited on devices handling client data, unless IT-approved and encrypted.
2.5 VPN must be used when accessing company systems from outside office premises.

=============================================================
SECTION 3: PASSWORD & AUTHENTICATION POLICY
=============================================================

3.1 All passwords must meet the following criteria:
    - Minimum 12 characters
    - Must include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and at least one special character
    - Must not contain the employee's name, username, or common dictionary words
3.2 Passwords must be changed every 90 days. The system will enforce this automatically.
3.3 Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all systems containing client data.
3.4 Employees must not share passwords with anyone, including IT support.
3.5 The use of password managers (approved list in Appendix A) is strongly encouraged.

=============================================================
SECTION 4: DATA CLASSIFICATION & HANDLING
=============================================================

4.1 Data is classified into four levels:
    - Public: Approved for external sharing
    - Internal: For employees only, not for clients
    - Confidential: Restricted to specific teams; requires encryption at rest and in transit
    - Restricted: Highest sensitivity; access requires specific role-based approval

4.2 Client data is always classified as Confidential or Restricted.
4.3 Employees must not download Confidential or Restricted data to personal devices.
4.4 All Confidential data transfers must use encrypted channels (HTTPS, SFTP, or approved encrypted email).

=============================================================
SECTION 5: INCIDENT REPORTING
=============================================================

5.1 Any suspected security breach, data loss, or unauthorized access must be reported to the IT Security team within 1 hour of discovery.
5.2 Failure to report a known security incident is a serious policy violation subject to disciplinary action.

NOTE [CONFLICT-007]: The Company Handbook (DOC-001) states incidents must be reported within 24 hours. This Information Security Policy (DOC-006) supersedes the handbook on security-specific timelines and requires reporting within 1 hour.

=============================================================
SECTION 6: ACCEPTABLE USE
=============================================================

6.1 Company systems and internet access are provided for business use. Limited personal use is permitted during breaks.
6.2 The following activities are strictly prohibited on company systems:
    - Accessing, downloading, or distributing illegal content
    - Installing unauthorized software
    - Circumventing security controls (e.g., VPN bypass, firewall bypass)
    - Mining cryptocurrency using company hardware
    - Sharing confidential client data on personal social media or messaging apps

SECTION 7: TRAINING REQUIREMENTS
=============================================================

7.1 All employees must complete the Information Security Awareness training within their first 5 business days.
7.2 Annual refresher training is mandatory for all staff.
7.3 Role-specific security training (e.g., Data Analyst — advanced data handling; Software Support Engineer — secure coding practices) is defined in the Role Requirement Matrix.
