NEXACORE SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
ROLE DESCRIPTION — HR EXECUTIVE
Document ID: DOC-015
Version: 1.0
Role ID: R03
Department: Human Resources

=============================================================
SECTION 1: ROLE SUMMARY
=============================================================

The HR Executive supports the Human Resources department in recruitment, employee relations, onboarding, performance management, and policy implementation.

=============================================================
SECTION 2: KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
=============================================================

- Coordinate end-to-end recruitment (job posting, screening, scheduling interviews, offer letters)
- Manage employee onboarding and offboarding processes
- Maintain accurate employee records in the HRMS
- Support the performance review cycle (biannual, June and December)
- Administer employee leave records and resolve leave disputes
- Conduct HR orientation sessions for new joiners
- Advise employees and managers on HR policies and procedures
- Handle confidential employee matters with discretion
- Support disciplinary and grievance procedures

=============================================================
SECTION 3: MANDATORY COMPETENCIES
=============================================================

- Thorough knowledge of NexaCore HR policies (HR Policy v2, DOC-003)
- Knowledge of Leave Policy v2 (DOC-005)
- Familiarity with Pakistani labor laws and SECP regulations
- HRMS proficiency (training required within 30 days)
- Confidentiality and data privacy (PECO Data Privacy Act compliance)
- Strong interpersonal and conflict-resolution skills

=============================================================
SECTION 4: MANDATORY ONBOARDING REQUIREMENTS
=============================================================

- Company orientation (Day 1)
- HR Policy v2 deep-dive training (Week 1-2)
- Leave Policy v2 training (Week 1)
- Recruitment and onboarding process training (Week 2-3)
- HRMS system training (Week 1-2)
- Data privacy and employee data handling training (Week 1)
- Information security awareness training (Week 1)
- Code of conduct and workplace conduct policy training (Week 1)
- Pakistani labor law overview session (Week 3)
- Supervised conduct of one full new-employee orientation session (Week 4)

=============================================================
SECTION 5: KPIs
=============================================================

- Time-to-fill ratio for open positions (target: under 30 days)
- New employee onboarding satisfaction score (target: 4.0/5.0)
- Leave record accuracy (audited quarterly)
- Performance review completion rate (target: 100%)
