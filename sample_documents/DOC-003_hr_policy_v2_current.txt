=============================================================
SECTION 1: RECRUITMENT & HIRING
=============================================================

1.1 All new hires must pass a background verification check before their start date.
1.2 Offer letters must be signed by the candidate, the HR Head, and the relevant Department Head.
1.3 New employees must submit the following on Day 1: CNIC copy, educational certificates, 2 passport-size photos, bank account details, and emergency contact information.
1.4 The company conducts reference checks with at least 2 previous employers before finalizing a hire.

=============================================================
SECTION 2: PROBATION PERIOD [CURRENT]
=============================================================

2.1 All employees undergo a 3-month probationary period (revised from 6 months in v1).
2.2 During probation, employees accrue leave at a rate of 1 day per month from Month 4 onwards.
2.3 Either party may terminate during probation with 7 days written notice.
2.4 At the end of probation, the line manager completes a Probation Review form and submits it to HR.
2.5 A satisfactory probation review is required before salary increment eligibility.

=============================================================
SECTION 3: COMPENSATION [CURRENT]
=============================================================

3.1 Salary increments are processed in January each year, effective from January 1st.
3.2 Increments are performance-based and range from 8% to 20% of base salary.
3.3 Overtime pay applies for hours worked beyond 48 hours per week at 1.5x the regular hourly rate.
3.4 Sales Executives and Branch Managers are eligible for commission and variable pay as per their compensation agreements.
3.5 Employees must submit timesheets by the 25th of each month.

=============================================================
SECTION 4: PERFORMANCE REVIEWS [CURRENT]
=============================================================

4.1 Formal performance reviews are conducted biannually: in June and in December.
4.2 Managers must submit completed KPI scorecards to HR at least 2 weeks before the review date.
4.3 Employees receive a written summary of their review outcomes within 5 business days.
4.4 Poor performance (below 50% on KPI scorecard) triggers a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) of 60 days.

=============================================================
SECTION 5: GRIEVANCE & DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURE
=============================================================

5.1 Minor infractions (first offense): verbal warning documented by HR.
5.2 Repeated or moderate infractions: written warning, HR file entry.
5.3 Serious infractions (data breach, misconduct, fraud): immediate suspension pending investigation; possible termination.
5.4 Employees may appeal disciplinary decisions to the HR Director within 5 business days.

=============================================================
SECTION 6: HEALTH & SAFETY
=============================================================

6.1 Workplace health and safety compliance is mandatory for all employees.
6.2 Any workplace accidents must be reported to HR and the Safety Officer within 24 hours.
