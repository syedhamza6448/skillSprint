ROLE DESCRIPTION — FINANCE ASSOCIATE
Document ID: DOC-016
Version: 1.0
Role ID: R04
Department: Finance & Accounts

=============================================================
SECTION 1: ROLE SUMMARY
=============================================================

The Finance Associate handles day-to-day financial operations including accounts payable, accounts receivable, expense processing, and financial reporting support.

=============================================================
SECTION 2: KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
=============================================================

- Log and process vendor invoices within 24 hours of receipt
- Coordinate payment approvals per Finance SOP authorization thresholds
- Issue client invoices within 3 business days of service delivery
- Monitor and follow up on overdue client payments
- Process employee expense reimbursements in the monthly payroll cycle
- Prepare data for monthly financial reports
- Ensure compliance with FBR tax filing requirements
- Maintain accurate financial records in the accounting system
- Support quarterly and annual audits

=============================================================
SECTION 3: MANDATORY COMPETENCIES
=============================================================

- Proficiency in NexaCore accounting software (training required within 30 days)
- Understanding of Finance SOP authorization thresholds and approval procedures
- Knowledge of FBR tax regulations (withholding tax, GST)
- Data accuracy and attention to detail
- Confidentiality in handling financial data

=============================================================
SECTION 4: MANDATORY ONBOARDING REQUIREMENTS
=============================================================

- Company orientation (Day 1)
- Finance & Accounts SOP training (Week 1-2)
- Accounting software training (Week 1-3)
- Tax compliance and FBR regulations training (Week 2-3)
- Payment authorization procedures and approval thresholds training (Week 1)
- Data privacy and financial data handling training (Week 1)
- Information security awareness training (Week 1)
- Code of conduct acknowledgment (Day 1)
- Month-end reporting process training (Week 3-4)
- Supervised processing of a complete month-end cycle (Week 4)

=============================================================
SECTION 5: KPIs
=============================================================

- Invoice processing time (target: 100% within 24 hours)
- Payment accuracy rate (target: 99.5%)
- Expense reimbursement cycle time (target: within payroll month)
- Tax filing compliance (target: 100% on-time)
