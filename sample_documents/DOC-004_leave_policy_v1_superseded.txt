=============================================================
SECTION 1: ANNUAL LEAVE [SUPERSEDED]
=============================================================

1.1 All employees are entitled to 10 days of annual leave per calendar year.

NOTE [VERSION-004]: SUPERSEDED. Leave Policy v2 (DOC-005) entitles employees to 14 days annual leave. Apply DOC-005.

1.2 Annual leave must be approved by the line manager at least 3 days in advance.
1.3 Unused annual leave can be carried forward for up to 6 months into the next year.

=============================================================
SECTION 2: SICK LEAVE [SUPERSEDED]
=============================================================

2.1 Employees are entitled to 7 days of sick leave per year.

NOTE [CONFLICT-005]: Leave Policy v2 (DOC-005) grants 10 days sick leave. DOC-005 supersedes this clause.

2.2 Sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered physician.

=============================================================
SECTION 3: MATERNITY / PATERNITY LEAVE [SUPERSEDED]
=============================================================

3.1 Female employees are entitled to 12 weeks (84 days) of paid maternity leave.

NOTE [VERSION-005]: Leave Policy v2 extends maternity leave to 16 weeks. DOC-005 supersedes this clause.

3.2 Paternity leave: 3 days paid leave for male employees upon birth of a child.

NOTE [CONFLICT-006]: Leave Policy v2 extends paternity leave to 7 days. DOC-005 supersedes this clause.

=============================================================
SECTION 4: PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
=============================================================

4.1 NexaCore observes all public holidays declared by the Government of Pakistan.
4.2 If a public holiday falls on a weekend, a compensatory day off is granted on the next working day.
