=============================================================
SECTION 1: RECRUITMENT & HIRING
=============================================================

1.1 All new hires must pass a background verification check before their start date.
1.2 Offer letters must be signed by both the candidate and the HR Head.
1.3 New employees must submit the following on Day 1: CNIC copy, educational certificates, 2 passport-size photos, bank account details.

=============================================================
SECTION 2: PROBATION PERIOD [SUPERSEDED]
=============================================================

2.1 All employees undergo a 6-month probationary period.

NOTE [VERSION-001]: This clause is SUPERSEDED. The current probation period is 3 months as per HR Policy v2 (DOC-003). Do not apply this 6-month figure.

2.2 During probation, no annual leave is accrued.

NOTE [CONFLICT-004]: HR Policy v2 (DOC-003) states that employees accrue leave from Month 4 onwards, even during the final months of probation. DOC-003 supersedes this clause.

=============================================================
SECTION 3: COMPENSATION [SUPERSEDED]
=============================================================

3.1 Salary increments are awarded once per year in July.

NOTE [VERSION-002]: HR Policy v2 (DOC-003) moved the increment cycle to January. Effective 2024, all increments are processed in January. This clause is superseded.

3.2 Overtime pay is applicable for hours worked beyond 48 hours per week, at 1.5x the regular hourly rate.

=============================================================
SECTION 4: LEAVE POLICY [SUPERSEDED — SEE DOC-004 AND DOC-005]
=============================================================

4.1 Annual Leave: 10 days per year (superseded — see DOC-005 for 14 days).
4.2 Sick Leave: 7 days per year with medical certificate required for absences exceeding 2 days.
4.3 Maternity Leave: 84 days (12 weeks) paid leave.

=============================================================
SECTION 5: PERFORMANCE REVIEW [SUPERSEDED]
=============================================================

5.1 Annual performance reviews are conducted in December each year.

NOTE [VERSION-003]: HR Policy v2 schedules reviews in June and December (biannual). This annual-only clause is superseded.
