ROLE DESCRIPTION — BRANCH MANAGER
Document ID: DOC-018
Version: 1.0
Role ID: R08
Department: Branch Management

=============================================================
SECTION 1: ROLE SUMMARY
=============================================================

The Branch Manager leads all operations for a NexaCore regional branch office, overseeing sales, support, operations, and staff management to meet branch-level revenue and performance targets.

=============================================================
SECTION 2: KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
=============================================================

- Oversee and manage all staff at the branch (Sales, Support, Operations)
- Drive branch revenue targets and report monthly to the Regional Director
- Conduct regular team performance reviews and mentor staff
- Ensure branch compliance with all NexaCore policies (HR, Finance, Security, Conduct)
- Manage branch-level client relationships and escalations
- Approve procurement and expense requests within branch authority limits
- Recruit and onboard branch staff (in coordination with HR)
- Submit monthly and quarterly branch performance reports
- Ensure branch facilities are maintained and operational

=============================================================
SECTION 3: MANDATORY COMPETENCIES
=============================================================

- Full knowledge of all NexaCore policies (HR, Finance, Security, Conduct)
- Leadership and people management (leadership training required within 30 days)
- P&L management and financial literacy (Finance SOP training within 30 days)
- Conflict resolution and disciplinary procedure management
- NexaCRM Pro dashboard and reporting proficiency

=============================================================
SECTION 4: MANDATORY ONBOARDING REQUIREMENTS
=============================================================

- Company orientation (Day 1)
- Full HR Policy v2, Leave Policy v2, and Finance SOP training (Week 1-2)
- Leadership and people management training (Week 2-3)
- Branch P&L and financial management training (Week 2-3)
- Information security leadership training (Week 1)
- Code of conduct acknowledgment and management responsibilities briefing (Day 1)
- Disciplinary and grievance procedure training (Week 2)
- NexaCRM Pro management dashboard training (Week 1-2)
- Regional Director and executive stakeholder introductions (Week 1)
- 30-day shadowing of the outgoing or interim Branch Manager (Weeks 1-4)

=============================================================
SECTION 5: KPIs
=============================================================

- Branch monthly revenue vs. target
- Staff retention rate at branch (target: below 15% annual turnover)
- Branch NPS/client satisfaction score
- Policy compliance audit score (target: 95%+)
